Why does your hunger come back at 4pm?

One tap. Then 60 seconds that might change how you see your weight.

steer

It's biology.

After a diet, your body turns up the hunger hormone ghrelin and turns down the hormones that make you feel full. In one study they were still changed a year later. That 4pm chai-and-biscuit pull is real.

Source: Sumithran et al., NEJM 2011

Medicines that work on this biology, called GLP-1 medicines, are everywhere now. So are the doubts.
